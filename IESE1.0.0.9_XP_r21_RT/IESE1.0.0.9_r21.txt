Tips:
=====
In the lists try to make a right click on the items or the first colum header to access other functionalities.
Right click is also used in other controls as in the portrait browser.

You can drag and drop items and spells from the browsers to the inventory or spell book 
or between the inventories or spellbooks (after opening several savegames).

You can't access main settings or change game choice without closing all documents.

You can use the resource browser to export game data including portraits.

In the proficiencies list (BG2), you can use '+' and respectively '-' keys to add and repectively remove 1 to the levels of the selected proficiencies. The 'DEL' key reset the level of the selected proficiencies. 
These keys change to 1st class levels, to change the 2nd class levels, the SHIFT key must be pressed simultaneously. To change both class levels at the same time, the 'CTRL' key must be pressed.

In the spell book (list of spells of the character), hiting 'CTRL' key while drag & dropping a spell from the spell brower or another spell book ADD the spell instead of REPLACING it in the case were the spell is dropped on a spell and not an empty area.

In the spell book, you can easily filter the spells, by right-clicking on the spell level & spell type column headers.
